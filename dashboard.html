Validation Methodology & Reporting Standards: Evaluated across 21 audited historical breach incidents (17 unique companies) using Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) and Leave-One-Group-Out Cross-Validation (LOGO-CV grouped by company to prevent cross-breach company leakage). The Historical Median Baseline achieves the lowest out-of-sample error (LOOCV MAE 3.66%, LOGO-CV MAE 3.72%) and is designated the primary benchmark. Random Forest (LOOCV MAE 4.18%) and Huber (LOOCV MAE 4.65%) are experimental comparison models. The 95% bootstrap confidence interval represents uncertainty in the mean MAE estimate across resamples, not an incident-level prediction interval.
